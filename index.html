About

Ekception

Ekception (Ek Chuchirdkiatskul) is a Bangkok-based DJ and producer whose sound has evolved around house and UK garage, while staying firmly rooted in disco, boogie, and funk.

After building a name across Bangkok's most respected club nights, he released his debut EP Wildlife (2020) — with the Luk Thung-inspired single Herbal Riddim featured on Mixmag Asia. In early 2020, he signed to Ballroom Records — founded by Thailand's most renowned house acts, Q Narongwate and Funky Gangster — releasing three records on the label.

His remixes and edits of Thai artists including Marsha, Mr. Z, Yellow Fang and Dharini Divari have become staples in local DJ sets, bridging international club culture with a distinctly Thai sensibility.
